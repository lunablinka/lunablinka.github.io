In a higher-education environment especially, advancement or donor relations can be critical for the growth of a university, college or department. 
There is a balance between instilling enough nostalgia to generate interest in giving and showing to a client’s audience that they are not stuck in the past.

It is important to portray mondern institutions as forward-thinking and poised to leverage donations into institutional growth that makes an institution a leader among peers. 

I am interested in stories that show societal impact and there is a lot of cross-over with narrative work here when searching for stories that are authentic and courageous in approach. 

<div style="padding:56.25% 0 0 0;position:relative;margin-bottom:20px;"><iframe src="https://player.vimeo.com/video/41365756?h=247702e5a9&loop=1&title=0&byline=0&portrait=0" style="position:absolute;top:0;left:0;width:100%;height:100%;" frameborder="0" allow="autoplay; fullscreen; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen></iframe></div>

In donor relations, vividly demonstraing the impact that a contribution can have, not only leads to significant impressions but often conversions.

Whether it is a well known alumnus returning to his alma mater, a discussion of history vs. the modern evolution of an institution, or a jubilant celebration of a university’s 125 anniversary: story matters.
