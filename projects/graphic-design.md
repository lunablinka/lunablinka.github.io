Graphic design, for me, is where a story lives beyond my film work.  Whether I am crafting a brand identity, designing print collateral, or working through editorial layout and typography, I approach every project with the same warmth and humanity that drives my work behind the camera. Good design should feel like it was made for a person, not a demographic. That belief informs every typographic choice and compositional decision I make. Design extends a story beyond the screen, giving it a physical presence that audiences can hold, wear, or walk past. When design and narrative work together, the result is something that resonates on a deeper emotional level.
