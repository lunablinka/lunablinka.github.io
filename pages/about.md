**MOTION** \| **STORYTELLING** \| **VISUAL DATA COMMUNICATION**

I am an interactive media producer with 20+ years experience in visual storytelling, integrated marketing for both high level ad agencies, and higher-education entities. I have a background in critical design thinking and creating interdisciplinary, multimedia communications. 

I have a bachelor's degree in industrial design and a master's degree in arts and liberal studies, with a focus on critical design, from North Carolina State University.
I have worked on campaigns that leverage visual communication techniques and critical analytics to create compelling, leading-edge stories that position clients as pioneers, explorers, thought-leaders, and stars.

I am interested in authentic communication.
My work seeks to elevate voices and stories that might otherwise go unnoticed.
Visual communication is a critical representation that connects and synthesizes a single, coherent story from a jumble of information. 

I work to meet media consumers wherever they are while providing paths to accessing complex ideas, imagery, and content.
I want clients to feel seen and to be made real and tangible for media consumers.

I strive to keep clients' stories at top of mind for consumers by leveraging the latest trends in media technologies, data analytics, and vertical marketing.
Every part of a campaign works synergistically to produce an experience that lingers in the memory of consumers and makes them curious to know more.
